title=[B42] Pass Through Window
description=Allows you to pass generators through open or broken windows.
description=
description=How it works:
description=- Equip a generator
description=- Right-click on an open or broken window
description=- Select "Pass [Generator] through window"
description=- The tile behind the window must be clear and walkable
description=- Your character will push the item through to the other side
description=
description=Note: Generators produce deadly fumes indoors. This mod is designed to move them onto rooftops or balconies that are only accessible through a window.
description=
description=Works with every generator, including those added by other mods, and with windows you built yourself. If you have suggestions for other items that would benefit from this mechanic, leave a comment!
description=
description=Compatibility:
description=- Requires Build 42.15 or later
description=- Localized in 12 languages: EN, DE, FR, ES, RU, PL, PT-BR, IT, TR, CN, KO, JP.
description=
description=Source: PassThroughWindow is MIT-licensed and fully open source.
description=https://github.com/armakupub/PassThroughWindow
