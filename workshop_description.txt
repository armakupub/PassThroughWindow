Pass Through Window

Allows you to pass generators through open or broken windows.

How it works:
- Equip a generator
- Right-click on an open or broken window
- Select "Pass [Generatortype] through window"
- The tile behind the window must be clear and walkable
- Your character will push the item through to the other side

Note: Generators produce deadly fumes indoors. This mod is designed to move them onto rooftops or balconies that are only accessible through a window.

Currently supports generators. If you have suggestions for other items that would benefit from this mechanic, leave a comment!

Compatibility:
- Requires Build 42.15 or later
- Localized in 12 languages: EN, DE, FR, ES, RU, PL, PT-BR, IT, TR, CN, KO, JP.
